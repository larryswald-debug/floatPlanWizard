## Authority and qualification controls

The Resources chapter preserves the production guide's links to U.S. Coast Guard boating-safety guidance, the U.S. Coast Guard Navigation Center, NOAA beacon registration, National Weather Service marine weather, National Park Service kayaking safety, U.S. Coast Guard Auxiliary vessel safety checks, and World Sailing offshore regulations. The book instructs readers to verify current requirements and conditions with the appropriate authority.

FloatPlanWizard is consistently described as a planning and communication aid. The manuscript explicitly states that it does not continuously watch every trip, verify distress, guarantee delivery or current position, contact authorities automatically, or dispatch assistance.
